# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone.

Participants are expected to use considerate language, respect differing viewpoints, accept constructive feedback, and focus on what is best for the community. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and unwelcome personal attention are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this code of conduct and may temporarily or permanently exclude participants for behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

For concerns, contact the project maintainers through the repository's private reporting channel.

Adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
